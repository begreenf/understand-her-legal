Understand Her Support

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

Contact

If something isn't working or you have a question, write to b.green@ecefyapp.com. Say what happened, on which screen, and which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Don't send screenshots or details of what you've logged: they aren't needed to help you. Everything you log stays on your iPhone, and the developer can't see it.

The basics

  • On the Home Screen the app is called UH.
  • It runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later, in English and Spanish.
  • In the app, Settings › How this works explains what it does and what it doesn't.

Premium subscription

  • Cancel or change your plan: in the app, Settings › Manage subscription (the row shows if you have Premium). Also in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Get back a subscription you already paid for: the app picks it up when it opens, as long as you're signed in to the App Store account you subscribed with. If it doesn't, go to Today or Guide, tap "See what Premium adds", then "Restore purchases".
  • Charges and refunds: Apple handles them. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. The developer doesn't see your payment details and can't reverse a charge.

Your data

  • What you log is stored only on your iPhone. This version doesn't sync with iCloud or with another device.
  • If you delete the app, what you logged is deleted. A backup of your iPhone may still contain it.
  • Delete everything: Settings › Your data › Delete everything. It can't be undone.
  • This version doesn't export the log to any file.
  • The developer can't recover your data: it's never sent to a developer server.

Face ID lock

  • Turn it on in Settings › Icon, notifications and Face ID › Require Face ID to open.
  • If Face ID doesn't recognize you, tap "Use iPhone passcode". The app has no password of its own, so there's nothing to reset by email.

Reminders

If they don't arrive:

  1. In the app, Settings › Icon, notifications and Face ID: check that "Receive reminders" is on, and check the time.
  2. In iPhone Settings › Notifications › UH: check that notifications are allowed.

On that same screen in the app you choose how much the reminder says on the lock screen: "Discreet", "With context" or "Full".

What this app isn't

Understand Her is not a medical, diagnostic or contraceptive tool. Everything it shows is an estimate based on what you log.

It isn't an emergency service either, and this email isn't monitored around the clock. If you or someone close to you is in danger, call your country's emergency number. In the app, Settings › Help and resources lists emergency numbers and helplines by country.
