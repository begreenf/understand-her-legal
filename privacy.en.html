Understand Her Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026.

Who publishes this app

Understand Her is published by Bernie Green, an individual, with no legal entity behind it.

Contact channel: to be confirmed. There isn't a dedicated email or other channel for privacy questions yet. This policy will be updated as soon as one exists.

What Understand Her is

Understand Her helps a person track and understand their partner's menstrual cycle and mood, based on a daily log that they enter into the app themselves. It is not a medical, diagnostic, or contraceptive tool.

Who uses the app, and whose data this is

Only the person who installs and uses the app is its user. Their partner has no account, does not access the app, and does not interact with it in any way. Everything the app records about that third person — moods, observations, period date — is entered by the app's user, not by her.

What data the app collects, and where it lives

The app stores, on the device itself:

  • The cycle dates and length the user enters.
  • An automatic estimate of the cycle phase for each day, calculated by the app itself. It's stored only as an internal audit record — the calendar, the guidance, and the learning engine always recalculate the phase again, they never read this stored value back.
  • An optional daily log, in closed lists of options (never free text):
    • The user's own mood, and whether they started or escalated an argument that day.
    • The mood they observe in their partner, and observations about her — this is where marking that her period started, for example, lives.
    • Whether there was an argument and, if so, its topic (money, family, plans, work, home, or other).
    • What else was going on that day, from a list shared by both sides of the log (work, money, family, health, sleep, plans, the cycle itself, or nothing in particular).
  • App settings: the chosen icon, notification preferences, and whether Face ID/Touch ID lock is on.

No name, photo, or any other data that identifies the partner is ever stored, either locally or on any server. The app has no free-text field at all, precisely so this cannot happen by accident.

How it's processed: entirely on the device, no exceptions

Understand Her has no server of its own. All the learning the app does about recorded patterns runs on the iPhone itself, without sending any data to any cloud service, without generative AI, and without the developer ever being able to see what a user records.

No analytics, no tracking

Understand Her does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking tool of any kind. It does not collect any data for purposes other than running the app itself on the user's device. This is formally declared to Apple in the app's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), which Apple reviews with every App Store submission.

Purchases and subscription

In-app purchases (the Premium subscription) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer never sees your card number or any other payment data — that information is handled directly by Apple, under its own privacy policy.

iCloud

Today, Understand Her does not sync data with iCloud: everything lives only on the device, and is lost if the app is uninstalled or the device is erased without a system-level backup. If iCloud sync is enabled in the future, it will be in each user's own private, encrypted container, and this policy will be updated before that happens to explain it.

No export

This version of the app does not allow exporting the log, in full or in part, to any format or destination. There is no share feature.

Your rights

Because all your data lives only on your device and the developer never receives or stores it on any server of their own, you exercise most of your rights directly from the app:

  • Access and rectification: you can view and correct any entry from within the app at any time.
  • Erasure: the "Delete everything" option in Settings permanently removes all your entries and cycle configuration from the device.
  • Portability: this version does not offer a feature to export your data to a file (see "No export" above).

If you have any other question about your data, you can write to the contact channel that will be added to this policy once it exists (see "Who publishes this app" above).

About your partner's data, specifically

Understand Her lets you record, for your own use only, patterns you observe in your partner's mood and cycle. That record:

  • Is entered by you, not by her. She has no account and no access to the app.
  • Stays entirely on your device. The developer of this app never receives, sees, or processes that record — there is no server it ever reaches.
  • Is not shared with, or exported to, anyone or any service (see "No export" above), and this version does not actively sync with iCloud (see "iCloud" above). If you back up your whole device — with iCloud or with your computer, as with any other app — that backup may include this record; it's managed by Apple or by your own computer, never by this app's developer.

This app's developer decides, for everyone alike, what kinds of things the app can record at all (the moods, observations, and topics in this policy, nothing more). But whether to record something specific about your partner on a given day, and what, is your own act, not the developer's — who never receives, sees, or processes that record (see above). If your partner has questions about something you've recorded about her, those questions are for you, not for the app's developer, who has no way to identify her or to access what you've recorded.

Children

Understand Her is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "Last updated" date above will be updated and, when the change warrants it, you'll also be notified inside the app itself.
